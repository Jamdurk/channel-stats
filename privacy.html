Privacy policy

Channel Stats is a private tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, read-only) to read statistics about the owner's own channel and videos: counts such as views, likes, shares, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and aggregated, anonymous audience breakdowns.

How the data is used and stored

  • The figures are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to show the owner how their videos perform.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.
  • No personal information about viewers is collected; YouTube only provides aggregated numbers.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in an encrypted store on the owner's computer.

Your rights

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
